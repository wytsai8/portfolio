About Me

I am a data-driven scientist working on machine learning and computational methods for biomedical data, including multi-omics and biomedical signal analysis.

I specialize in transforming complex, high-dimensional biomedical data into actionable insights using statistical and machine learning methods. My work spans biomedical imaging signals, genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, next-generation sequencing, and clinical datasets.

During my PhD, I focused on Alzheimer’s disease biomarker discovery using multi-modal proteomics and clinical data, as well as computational modeling of biomedical imaging signals for biological parameter prediction. I emphasize scalable data processing, model development, and interpretability in collaboration with interdisciplinary research teams.

I am particularly interested in applying my expertise in bioinformatics, healthcare analytics, machine learning, and data-driven problem solving in cross-functional environments.
